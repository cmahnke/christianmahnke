Not much could be found out about her: she was born in 1878 as Anna Schrutz and studied at the [School of Applied Arts in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Arts,_Architecture_and_Design_in_Prague) [^1]. There, she also took part in [several exhibitions](https://databazevystav.udu.cas.cz/en/person?db=umelci&searchsurname=Schrutz&searchname=Anna). She was a member of the [Metznerbund](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metznerbund) (all links in German), an association of German-speaking artists in Czechoslovakia, as well as the [Association of German Visual Artists in Bohemia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%BCnstlervereinigungen_deutschsprachiger_K%C3%BCnstler_aus_B%C3%B6hmen,_M%C3%A4hren_und_Schlesien_vor_1945#Verein_deutscher_bildender_K%C3%BCnstler_in_B%C3%B6hmen_(1895%E2%80%931920)) and the [German-Bohemian Artists’ Federation](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%BCnstlervereinigungen_deutschsprachiger_K%C3%BCnstler_aus_B%C3%B6hmen,_M%C3%A4hren_und_Schlesien_vor_1945#Deutschb%C3%B6hmischer_K%C3%BCnstlerbund_(1908%E2%80%931920)). She also designed bookplates and posters.

The picture shows a scene in a Dutch harbour. This is evident from the type of boat (flat-bottomed boat) and the traditional costumes, such as the klompen (wooden clogs).
The location is thought to be the harbour on the island of [Marken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marken) in the [Zuiderzee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuiderzee) (now the [Markermeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markermeer)) ([approximate location on GeoHack](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?params=52.458508_N_5.101093_E)).
Comparative images can be found in the [Zuiderzee Collection](https://www.zuiderzeecollectie.nl/) and on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Havenbuurt_(Marken)). One example is [the houses on the right-hand side](https://www.zuiderzeecollectie.nl/object/collect/Zuiderzee_museum-36792). A [further image from 1916](https://www.zuiderzeecollectie.nl/object/collect/Zuiderzee_museum-36199) shows a denser development, suggesting that the illustration was probably created earlier.

From the end of the 19th century onwards, the island was frequently visited by artists.

[^1]: [Anniversary exhibition of the Artists’ Association for Bohemia in Prague, 1908](https://databazevystav.udu.cas.cz/archiv/exhib_cz/1908_k_nj.pdf#page=58)
