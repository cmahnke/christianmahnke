Mehmet Güler is a Turkish-German artist who was born in Malatya (Turkey) in 1944 and studied art in Ankara and Kassel. He has been living and working in Kassel as a painter, graphic artist and illustrator since the 1970s.

The print is entitled "Der Einzug" (The Arrival) and dates from 1985. It is an artist’s proof (EA - Épreuve d’Artiste).
