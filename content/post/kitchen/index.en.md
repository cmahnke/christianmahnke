Having already renovated my living room at the start of the year, it was now the kitchen"s turn.
<!--more-->

About 10 months after the [last renovation](/en/post/living-room/), I now had all the bits and pieces I needed for my kitchen.

The main suppliers were (once again)
* [Zeitzeug](http://zeitzeug.de/) for the period wallpaper and some of the certificates.
* [Möbelino](https://www.moebelino.de/) for the main part of the kitchen

The fridge is a classic: a [Bosch, Type 140 S-N](https://www.bavarikon.de/object/bav:MOS-OBJ-0000000000025467?lang=en). The handles on the wall cabinet, with their rounded shape, match the rest of the colourful scheme, so I assume they’re from the same range. I managed to snap both of them up on eBay Kleinanzeigen.

I bought the [Langnese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Magnum_Ice_Cream_Company) sign at a local flea market. It features the [Langnese logo used until 1998](https://logos.fandom.com/wiki/Langnese).
