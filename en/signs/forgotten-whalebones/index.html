The forgotten whale bones of Göttingen

The forgotten whale bones of Göttingen

This historical artefact is half a lower jawbone of a baleen whale, as can be seen from the fibrous surface structure characteristic of bone and the porous internal structure (cancellous bone). The other half was also still in existence until at least 2022.

Arches made from baleen whale lower jaws were produced mainly in the 18th and 19th centuries as “souvenirs” from whaling expeditions of the time. They are typically found in coastal regions and port cities in northern Germany and northern Europe that were once centres of whaling.

As the date of formation and the exact origin of this natural monument – which is unusual for southern Lower Saxony – have not yet been established, it can be described as Göttingen’s forgotten whale bones.
