Abstract painting of unknown origin

Friday, November 27, 2026

I found an abstract painting by an artist I hadn’t heard of before…

I like the colors and the sense of movement created by the arrangement of the colored areas.

Painting
Close-up
Back

Unfortunately, the drawing is not signed. Pencil lines marking the edges are visible, as well as white paint applied to straighten the edges (see close-up). Unfortunately, there are no further details on the back. However, the discoloration of the cardboard and the strips of adhesive tape suggest that the drawing was framed at least 40 to 50 years ago.

The motif is an abstracted clock face that follows a gentle spiral (from the inside—at 1 o’clock—to the outside—at 12 o’clock).

Incidentally, the painting has so far eluded AI-based analysis, as no model has yet been able to recognize the motif.
