Colour woodcut from Eva Roemer (1889-1977)

Sunday, March 19, 2023
Updated on
Friday, January 2, 2026

I found a woodcut by Eva Roemer (1889-1977) (at artnet)…

The title is probably “Snow on the reeds (Lake Starnberg)” (German: “Schnee auf dem Schilf (Starnberger See)”).

Print
Signature

Since I have been working with Gerbrand Caspers for years, this article is another good opportunity to refer to his work on Eva Roemer:

  • The Linosaurus: Eva Roemer
  • DAS HAUS DER FRAU: Eva Roemer](https://www.dashausderfrau.nl/roemer-eva.html) - here you can also find the print above.

Eva Roemer was also associated with Göttingen (she lived in the town from 1914 to 1926), further information on her can be found in Göttinger Künstlerlexikon by Thomas Appel.

Update 2 January 2026

The Museum Starnberger See dedicated an exhibition to her from 28 March to 26 October 2025 (https://www.museum-starnberger-see.de/ausstellungen/aktuell/eva-roemer).
