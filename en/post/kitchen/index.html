Kitchen refurbished

Tuesday, November 16, 2021

Having already renovated my living room at the start of the year, it was now the kitchen"s turn.

About 10 months after the last renovation, I now had all the bits and pieces I needed for my kitchen.

The main suppliers were (once again)

  • Zeitzeug for the period wallpaper and some of the certificates.
  • Möbelino for the main part of the kitchen

The fridge is a classic: a Bosch, Type 140 S-N. The handles on the wall cabinet, with their rounded shape, match the rest of the colourful scheme, so I assume they’re from the same range. I managed to snap both of them up on eBay Kleinanzeigen.

I bought the Langnese sign at a local flea market. It features the Langnese logo used until 1998.
