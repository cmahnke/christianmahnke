Quantum cosmos Göttingen

A digital exhibit
Wednesday, March 26, 2025
Updated on
Thursday, March 27, 2025
DOI of the presentation: 10.5281/zenodo.18618940

Today (26 March) is the opening of the exhibition ‘Was zum Quant?!’ in the Forum Wissen. You can also see a digital exhibit in which I was involved…

The installation “Quantum cosmos Göttingen” shows the places where quantum physicists lived and worked in Göttingen from the summer semester of 1921 to the winter semester of 1936/36. The underlying city map is freely scalable, and the timeline at the bottom allows you to select the semester. Comparable to Europeana.4D, GeoTemCo, or the Dariah GeoBrowser…

The data was collected by Pia Denkmann, the geodata enrichment, JSON transformation and the web frontend are by me. The texts were written by Christine Nawa and Ramona Dölling. The integration into the exhibition was carried out by the agency Cognitio.

Screenshot of the prototype

Technical details

The map was georeferenced using QGIS.

The coordinates of the addresses are determined using Nominatim from the OpenStreetMap project.
